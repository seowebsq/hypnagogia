about

hypnagogia is a voxel sandbox set inside a dream that will not hold still. some features include:

  • a lucidity meter that bends everything: colours drift, terrain breathes, gravity flips by region, and blocks behind you quietly stop existing.
  • crafting with words instead of recipes — carve WARM, BACKWARDS and LOUD out of the dream and fuse them into blocks. half-dreaming, the dream may mishear you.
  • three dream shards locked behind singing doors that only open for the right melody, stepped out on loud blocks. a frog knows the songs. don't meet its eyes.
  • a stairway of almost-nothing to an island that exists only while you are dreaming, crowned by a dreamfruit that wakes you just enough to drop you.
  • the world remembers: everything you build comes back later, elsewhere, wrong — even across sessions.
  • in deep dream the inventory lies, the sky is a slowly wheeling eye or the inside of a fruit, and a moth may steal the words from your mouth.

how to play

  • WASD move · mouse or arrows look · space jump
  • left click breaks · right click places · Q cycles your words
  • find the three shards and carry them to the pink anchor bed to wake for good
  • the bed restores lucidity — hitting zero rearranges the dream around you

faq

do i need to install anything?
no. it runs in any modern browser, on a single html file.
is my inventory really lying to me?
only when you are deep enough in the dream. your items are safe. probably.
i lost a word.
the dream keeps one as toll each time you wake. the moth gives doubles back, if you can catch it.
will there be updates?
the dream keeps rewriting itself. so yes.
